# Letter of Intent

**From:** _______________ (“Party A”)  
**To:** _______________ (“Party B”)

This letter of intent (this “Letter”) sets out the parties’ shared understanding that they intend, in good faith, to explore a possible arrangement relating to certain matters of mutual interest (the “Proposed Arrangement”).

## 1. Purpose

The parties wish to continue discussions and, if appropriate, work toward a more detailed understanding of whether a mutually acceptable arrangement can be reached. This Letter is intended only as a high-level statement of that intent.

## 2. Proposed Arrangement

The Proposed Arrangement may involve one or more forms of collaboration, engagement, or other commercial or operational relationship, as the parties may later determine. The scope, structure, timing, and other material terms remain to be discussed and are not agreed by this Letter.

## 3. Next Steps

Subject to each party’s continued interest, the parties currently anticipate that they may:

- continue discussions at a reasonable pace;
- exchange such information as each party considers appropriate; and
- consider whether to negotiate a definitive written agreement.

None of the foregoing is required, and either party may change this process at any time.

## 4. Non-Binding Effect

This Letter is not a contract, offer, or commitment. It does not create any binding obligation, including any obligation to continue discussions, enter into a definitive agreement, or consummate a transaction of any kind.

No legally binding agreement will exist unless and until the parties execute a subsequent written agreement that expressly states it is binding. Either party may discontinue discussions at any time, for any reason or for no reason, without liability to the other.
